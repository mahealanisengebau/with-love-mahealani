Letters to Izzy

September 2026 · Age 2

Dear Izzy,

As of right now, you are 2 years old and you are the bravest, silliest, smartest 2-year-old I have ever known. You make friends so easily and you're so extroverted. Before I had you, I always kept to myself when in public and didn't greet people I don't know, but the way you are so openly kind to strangers broke me out of my shell.

You are so brave! When we went to the clinic to get your 2-year-old shots, you braced yourself for your shots by saying, "one more mesisin!" through tears, and took it like a champ! As we were leaving you even said, "Thank you, nurse!" without me even telling you to do so. You never cease to amaze me, Izzy.

Right now, you laugh at the silliest things like fart jokes (elut ra Tomiko btw), stinky smells, funny animals, and just find anything humorous. You brighten up my days with your silly jokes and you really never stop talking from the second you wake up to the moment you go to bed. Ke mal di ua Angko rkau ra Ombong el mal meduch a ngerel. Mommy Tomi calls it “24/7 podcast with Izzy”. Sometimes I get overstimulated when you talk a lot but your laugh is also so contagious and you're so genuinely funny I can never stay mad for too long.

Some days I get dysregulated, and I yell and get impatient with you, but I try to repair it right away and apologize when I do something wrong. I hope it never gets to a point where it affects your development. Because of you, Isabelle, I have learned a new meaning of what love means to me and it is everything in Corinthians.

Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy, it does not boast, it is not proud. It does not dishonor others, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs. Love does not delight in evil but rejoices with the truth. It always protects, always trusts, always hopes, always perseveres. Love never fails.

1 Corinthians 13:4–8

Because of you, I have learned a new perspective of patience and kindness, and I strive to bring that love that you've taught me into all the relationships in my life. I may fail, but I never stop trying to be better for you because you give me every reason to be a better person.

You've taught me so much more than I could have ever anticipated, and you deserve the world. All the words in every language will never suffice.

Love,
Mommy
